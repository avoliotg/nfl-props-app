## 1. THE CENTRAL FINDING, AND WHY IT RESOLVES A LONG-STANDING TENSION

**Every beta in this project is measured against the LINE. The line is the
stale quantity. The price is the sharp one.**

Receptions is 93 percent flat on the line because FanDuel moves reception
prices through the ODDS. So beta, which regresses `actual - line` on
`projection - line`, spends both of its terms on the quantity that barely
moves and discards the one carrying the market's information.

Consequence, measured in `receptions_classifier.py`:

| design | out-of-sample log loss | delta vs market |
|---|---|---|
| market (devigged book_p) | 0.68207 | n/a |
| A book_p only | 0.68275 | +0.00068 |
| B book_p + line | 0.68311 | +0.00104 |
| C book_p + line + features | 0.68320 | +0.00113 |
| D book_p + line + dev | 0.68308 | +0.00101 |
| E line + features (no book_p) | 0.69025 | +0.00818 |

Every design is WORSE than the market. The encompassing test (the primary,
see §6) gives t values of 0.69, 1.38, 1.12, 1.45, 1.24, nothing near 2. The
`book_p` coefficient is about 1.17, so the market is well calibrated with no
slack to exploit.

**This is a powered null, not an underpowered one.** Measured on synthetic
data at this sample size (860 games, 9 props each), the encompassing test
returns t +7.45 when a real advantage is planted. See §6 for why the naive
tests would have misled.

So beta 0.226 with t +6.5 is REAL and it is real against the stale quantity.
Both facts are true and they are no longer in conflict.

### receiving: YES, narrower and softer.

**Rule:** FanDuel, `receiving_air_yards` recent-3 minus base-8, z-scored
within market, `z >= 1.0`, bet the UNDER, **skip the top `devz` quintile**.
~250 bets/season. In-sample ROI ~+0.09.

From `air_yards_retest.py`, lift against the correct baseline (the market's
own under ROI, not zero):

| market / input | z≥0.5 | z≥1.0 | z≥1.5 | z≥2.0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| receiving / air_yards | +0.043 | +0.086 | +0.113 | +0.148 |
| receptions / air_yards | +0.059 | +0.070 | +0.083 | +0.075 |
| receptions / air_yards_share | +0.044 | +0.096 | +0.062 | +0.061 |
| receiving / targets | +0.017 | +0.063 | +0.088 | +0.105 |

12 of 40 cells clear. Receiving shows clean monotone dose-response.
Invariant across five definitions of agreement (+0.086 to +0.107).

**Direction is the OPPOSITE of the original hypothesis.** Not underreaction
to a bad narrative; the line **OVERreacts to recent downfield usage**.

**Mechanism, and it is fragile** (`air_yards_retest.py` §3). The market's
weight is FLAT (line~recent: 61.5, 53.7, 59.1) while the TRUE predictive
weight COLLAPSED (actual~recent: 56.6, 33.6, 37.5). FanDuel kept pricing air
yards the same while it stopped predicting yards as well. **A stale market
weight can un-stale itself**, so this is a fact about a two-year window
rather than a structural bias.

**The model's role is to VETO, not confirm** (`conjunction_followup.py`). The
`devz` quintile shape inside the rule cell is flat then collapses:
+0.1001, +0.0790, +0.1266, +0.0859, **-0.1171**. So the model identifies one
bad bucket (top quintile, projection well above line) and adds nothing
directional elsewhere. Every dummy coefficient is insignificant (t 0.85,
0.74, -0.63) while `z` stays strong (t 2.84–2.96).

**Best-of-grid placebo: p = 0.072 and 0.065.** Borderline. And season ROI
decays +0.165 → +0.102 → +0.070.

### qb_passing: YES, thinnest, and it means something different than it looks.

**`qb_passing_sidepicker.py` + `qb_passing_followup.py`.** Walk-forward
logistic on the module's five features. ROI profile from threshold 0.02 up:
+0.024, +0.028, +0.006, +0.057, +0.084, +0.132. Best-of-grid placebo
**p = 0.010** on both statistics.

**CRITICAL REFRAMING.** `book_p` in this market has **standard deviation
0.00058 across five distinct values**; 61.2 percent of props are -110/-110
and 30.9 percent -112/-112. Dropping `book_p` entirely moves the slope from
+0.7776 to +0.7739 and leaves ROI and bet count byte-identical. So this is
**NOT** a signal from disagreeing with a price. FanDuel puts all of its
qb_passing information in the LINE, and the model has skill at calling which
side of it the outcome lands.

**The principle this establishes, and it is the most transferable thing in
the session:** qb_passing beta is 0.085 with a CI spanning zero, so the
projection adds nothing on a yards scale, yet the side-picker works.
**Calling the SIGN of a threshold crossing is an easier problem than
improving the conditional mean.**

**Caveats.** Only 2024 and 2025 are scorable (2023 has nothing before it), so
two seasons: +0.0301 and +0.1519 at threshold 0.06, meaning the pooled figure
is mostly one season. And ROI decays threefold as the cell floor rises
(+0.0839 at 200 bets → +0.0259 at 400) even though p holds, so the absolute
edge is concentrated in narrow cells.

**Also retires a recorded figure:** the plan's "-113/-114, ~6.1 percent hold"
is wrong for qb_passing. Modal price is -110.

**Known gap, worth one test:** the design OMITS the line. Since the target is
P(Y > L), the model was inferring the threshold from correlated features. A
one-word change and it should only help.

### receptions: NO. Signal without a side-picker.

Only market with a demonstrated projection signal (beta 0.226) and the
forecasting test above says it adds nothing to the price. Air-yards rule does
not clear here (+0.0366, CI spans zero; z≥1.5 flips signs). Plan 5.1
candidate rule still carries its threshold-discontinuity problem.

### qb_rushing: NO, but measured for the first time.

Beta 0.094 at FanDuel, CI [-0.086, +0.273]. Reference only.

**The 0.655 consensus figure was an artifact.** `qb_rushing_anchor.py` traced
it: on the 1,380 props where FanDuel posted, consensus beta is 0.098 versus
FanDuel's 0.094, so no anchor effect at all. The entire divergence came from
325 props FanDuel never posted, where consensus beta is 0.956 (t +12.09) and
2023's 176 rows give 0.987 (t +18.16, indistinguishable from exactly 1).
**Mechanism:** when the line is uninformative noise with high variance,
beta → 1 mechanically (`beta = [cov(p,a) + var(L)] / [var(p) + var(L)]`).
Confirmed because observed 0.987 EXCEEDS the unanchored actual~proj slope of
0.931, which only the high-variance regime can produce.

**New audit rule:** a prop with no FanDuel line must be excluded from any
fit. It is unbettable AND it injects a beta artifact.

### anytime_td: NO, and structurally excluded.

One-sided pricing, so two-sided devigging is impossible and none of the
under-side work applies.

## 5. MEASURED FACTS THAT CORRECT THE PLAN

**FanDuel's hold is LOWER than recorded.** Measured median: **4.55 percent on
yardage markets, 5.73 on receptions**. The plan says 6.1 and 6.9. Two errors
compounded: 6.1 is the OVERROUND at -113 rather than the hold, and FanDuel
does not price yardage at -113 anyway. **At a 4.55 percent hold the symmetric
price is -110.**

**OPEN ITEM, ONE LINE:** `eval_harness` hardcodes breakeven **0.5305 (-113)**
for all five markets. The correct yardage figure is **0.5238**. Four of five
markets have been judged against a bar 0.67 points too high. Receptions at
0.5304 happens to be right.

**Shin ≡ additive for a two-outcome market.** Verified to 1e-13 across 20,000
random price pairs. So there are THREE distinct devig methods, not four, and
the literature's preference for Shin over multiplicative reduces here to a
preference for equal absolute margin over proportional.

**Method disagreement is negligible where you bet.** Spread in fair P(over):
0.18 points at +102/-122, 0.30 at -130/+104, up to 2.07 at +920/-1400. So
receptions at 2.5/3.5 is nearly method-independent, and the candidate rule's
+0.071 book component is probably NOT a devigging artifact.

**The market is well calibrated.** `market_calibration.py`: across
qb_passing, qb_rushing, receiving and receptions, 28 of 28 book-market cells
show the under below breakeven. Only rushing's 7 cells are positive.

**The harness's "vs breakeven" column only ever tested the OVER.** A deficit
on the over is a surplus on the under, and nobody flipped it. That is how the
rushing finding was missed for three sessions.

**The harness gap is per-market, not one number** (`qb_beta_compare.py`).
`eval_harness` does not call `load_model`, but for `qb_rushing` the refit and
the shipped model are **identical by construction** (`season < S` and
`season <= S-1` are the same set, and `load_model` applies no training gate).
The gap exists only where `load_model` does something the harness does not
replicate: `rushing` gates `carries_roll >= 1.5`, `receptions` and
`receiving` gate `targets_roll >= 3`.

## 6. METHODOLOGY LESSONS (these generalise; several cost real time today)

**A t-statistic is not a test when a threshold was chosen by argmax.** The
qb_passing side-picker's original fragility (248 bets/+0.1278 → 194/+0.0996
on a TWO-ROW input change) was never a data problem. Two rows cannot move a
bet count by 54 except through a selection step. **Third independent sighting**
of this defect after the receptions candidate rule's argmax and its impossible
7–15 point bin discontinuity.

**Use a PLACEBO for best-of-N, not Bonferroni**, when cells overlap. Permute
the signal within stratum, recompute the whole grid, take the max, repeat.
That p-value IS corrected for the selection.

**Placebos need a cell-size floor and a t-based statistic.** First version
returned **p = 0.160 on a genuinely real signal** because a 104-bet cell
dominated the null. With cells under 200 excluded and a t statistic:
**p = 0.037 real, p = 0.960 noise**.

**A jagged profile does NOT prove absence.** On synthetic data with a real
signal (slope +0.546, t +2.70) the ROI profile still had two sign changes.
The informative quantity is the COUNT of positive thresholds and the placebo
p, not smoothness.

**The simple disagreement slope is uninformative once the price varies.**
Measured: t +0.62 when the model was genuinely better. Use the ENCOMPASSING
form, `outcome ~ book_p + (p_model - book_p)`, which gives t +7.45 on the same
data. qb_passing's +0.7739 was interpretable ONLY because `book_p` was
constant there.

**Encompassing and log loss answer different questions.** A worse forecast can
carry independent information, so a positive encompassing coefficient means
"adds to the market", not "beats the market". Report both.

**The binary agree/disagree split can return the WRONG SIGN.** With signals
correlated at 0.55, the planted-real world gave -0.0392 and the planted-noise
world gave -0.1483, both negative. Use the continuous form.

**Sign consistency is a weak bar when magnitudes differ fourfold.** Air yards
"passed" three seasons at -4.92, -20.05, -21.62. That is present in two,
absent in one.

**Exclude partial seasons from a sign vote.** A 335-row 2026 was vetoing six
findings measured on ~2,600-row seasons.

**Score cells against the right BASELINE, not zero.** An under cell inherits
the structural under bias. Receptions air-yards-share at z≥1.0 looked like
+0.0742 against zero but is +0.096 against the market's -0.0221 baseline.

**Correlated inputs are one test, not six.** Air yards / air-yards share /
WOPR / targets / target share correlate 0.69–0.94. Six flags ≈ two findings.
Cuts both ways: smaller multiplicity penalty, less independent evidence.

**`(actual - line) ~ X` gives exactly the difference of coefficients from
`actual ~ X` and `line ~ X`.** Verified to 1e-13. Bootstrapping that
difference is unnecessary machinery; clustered SEs are exact.

**Reading a CRLF file with `encoding='utf-8'` silently converts `\r\n` to
`\n`.** This broke three consecutive patch attempts. Read binary, decode,
patch, write binary. The asserts are what prevented file corruption, so keep
asserting match counts before writing.

**A tail-replacement patch can delete functions you did not intend.** I
replaced everything from `_qb_player_stats` onward in `qb_rushing.py` and
silently deleted `build_upcoming_week`, which `project_week` calls. **Always
diff the function inventory after patching a file.**

**`pip freeze > requirements.txt` is dangerous.** It added 71 transitive pins
built for Python 3.14 (Community Cloud runs older) and **silently dropped
`openpyxl`**, which the app needs. The hand-maintained unpinned list was
already correct. Reverted.

## 8. THINGS THAT WOULD BE MISTAKES TO TRY

**GAMLSS or neural nets for the projection layer.** Two reasons. Sigma shifts
all probabilities uniformly, never flipping a side, and skew is already
understood as distributional rather than an edge. More decisively, the honest
blend beats the line by **0.97 percent of variance in receptions and 2.6
percent in receiving**, and that is the entire room available to any functional
form. And 22,540 rows with 6 tabular features is where gradient boosting
beats neural nets reliably.

**The better version of that instinct:** the models do NOT use air yards, and
air yards is the one input measured as misweighted by the market, surviving
the control for the projection. Feature addition beats estimator change.

**Pricing honesty (worth doing, distinct from side-picking):** the harness
reports `resid_sd` per market (receiving 28.4, receptions 2.1, rushing 30.6,
qb_passing 72.7, qb_rushing 17.1) as the correct sigma around a blended mean,
while the shipped sigma was fitted around the projection and includes
projection error. That is a recalibration, not new machinery.

## 11. POST-PUSH HOTFIX (after `a293c6a`)

**The deploy crashed on first load. Local runs all passed. Cause:**

`norm_join_name` used `r"[\u0300-\u036f]"` for accent folding. Being a RAW
string, the regex engine received the six literal characters `\u0300`.
Python's `re` understands that escape; **RE2 does not** and raises
`ArrowInvalid: invalid regular expression: invalid escape sequence: \u`.

pandas backs a string column with **pyarrow** when pyarrow is available, and
`.str.replace(regex=True)` is then executed by RE2 rather than by `re`.
Locally the column was object dtype, so `re` ran and everything passed.
Streamlit Cloud had pyarrow, so RE2 ran and `receiving.build_dataset` died at
the first `norm_join_name` call, taking the whole app down.

**Two fixes, both in `models/data_utils.py`:**

1. Dropped the `r` prefix, so Python resolves the escapes and the engine
   receives the two literal combining characters. Line 192's class was
   already non-raw and was never affected.
2. **Pinned the engine.** `norm_join_name` now converts to object dtype
   before any regex work:
   `pd.Series(s.astype(str).to_numpy(dtype=object), index=s.index)`.
   This closes the whole class of RE2-versus-`re` divergence rather than the
   one escape that happened to surface.

**`smoke_norm_name.py` now exercises the Arrow backend.** It runs all 28
cases through a `dtype="string[pyarrow]"` Series and compares against the
object-dtype output, skipping with a printed NOTE when pyarrow is absent.

**The methodology lesson, and it is the important part.** The smoke rig had 28
cases and full coverage of the accent and tag rules, and it still missed this,
because the rig and the failure differed in DTYPE rather than in input. The
container the code was written in had no pyarrow; the deploy target did.
**A test that never exercises the production backend is not a test of
production.** Any future string-processing change should be checked against
both dtypes, which that rig now does automatically on any machine with
pyarrow.

## 19. ADDITIONAL METHODOLOGY LESSONS FROM PART TWO

**Read the docstring of the function you are calling.** `blended_mean` said
"Pass the result to p_over, not the raw projection" and I passed the raw
projection anyway, then reported a null about a dead code path.

**Never wrap a call you are unsure of in a bare `try/except` that substitutes
a sentinel.** It converts a signature error into a data property. Discover the
signature with `inspect.signature` and PRINT any exception.

**Abort on an empty frame.** A gate that passes on zero rows is worse than no
gate.

**A diagnostic that hardcodes the defect cannot verify the fix.** Keep both:
the hardcoded characterisation for stability, and a section that reads the
shipped module.

**A self-test asserting a snapshot will block a legitimate change.** Assert
the invariant plus an explicit expected list.

**Reading a CRLF file with `encoding='utf-8'` converts `\r\n` to `\n`
silently**, so multi-line patch patterns never match. Read binary, decode,
patch, write binary. The asserts before the write are what prevented
corruption across roughly six failed patch attempts today.

**Check the function inventory after any tail-replacement patch.** I deleted
`build_upcoming_week` from `qb_rushing.py` that way and shipped a version that
would have crashed every board render.

**A constant column looks like information.** The model's P(over) was
identical for every player on four boards for an unknown period and nobody
noticed, because a number carries authority a blank does not.

**`pip freeze > requirements.txt` is actively dangerous.** It added 71
transitive pins built for Python 3.14 (Cloud runs older) and **silently
dropped `openpyxl`**, which the app needs. matplotlib was already in the
hand-maintained list; only the local venv was missing it.
